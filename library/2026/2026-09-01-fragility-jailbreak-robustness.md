# The Fragility of Jailbreak Robustness Across Operational States

## Notes

Vanilla-state ASR does not characterize jailbreak robustness: changing only an ordinary (non-adversarial) system prompt can swing ASR dramatically (one case +56 pp, 2%→58%). Seven models, three attacks; state shifts track a refusal-related representation axis. EMNLP 2026 Findings.

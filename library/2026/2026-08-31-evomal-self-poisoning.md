# EvoMal: Self-Poisoning in Self-Evolving Coding Agents

## Notes

Self-evolving coding agents that imitate retrieved skills can re-author a planted malicious skill (never invoked) into their own library. Copies persist after the seed is removed. ASPR 20.3–41.8% across six models on 153 SWE-bench Verified tasks; targeted descriptions raise it to 86.7%; Qwen3 keeps 68% ASPR at round 5 after seed removal. Counter-prompt cuts ASPR to ≤6.7% with no significant task-completion loss.

# The Fragility of Jailbreak Robustness Across Operational States

## Notes

Jailbreak robustness measured as a single vanilla-state ASR is fragile: holding the attack fixed and changing only an ordinary, safety-irrelevant system prompt can shift ASR dramatically (e.g. 2% → 58%). Across seven aligned models and three attacks (PAIR, LAA, AutoDAN), 18/21 model–attack pairs had at least one non-vanilla state with higher ASR than vanilla. State-dependent shifts track a refusal-related representation axis. Argues evaluations need robustness stability across operational states, not only vanilla ASR.
